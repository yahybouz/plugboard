# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: we want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful and constructive, welcome newcomers, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at the link above and applies to every project space: issues, merge requests, discussions and any other channel used for the project.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, open a **confidential issue** in the project's GitLab repository, addressed to the maintainers. Reports are handled privately, and maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reports.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the code of conduct may be removed from the project.
